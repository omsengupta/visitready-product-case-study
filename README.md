# VisitReady
*A product case study for helping patients prepare for routine appointments.*

## Problem
Some patients may find it difficult to remember what they want to discuss
during an appointment or keep track of the next steps afterward.
This is a hypothesis to investigate, not a research finding.

## Target users
Adults managing their own routine primary-care appointments.

## Research plan
I would interview a small group of people about how they prepare for appointments
and remember follow-up steps. I would not collect private medical details.

## Proposed solution
A simple checklist for questions before a visit and a notes area afterward
for recording instructions from the clinician.

## MVP
- Add questions before an appointment
- Review the list during the visit
- Record clinician-provided next steps afterward

## Success measures
- People who complete a pre-visit checklist
- People who record a next step after a visit
- How confident users feel about remembering what to do next

## Safety and privacy
This concept does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or replace clinician advice.
Do not add real patient information to this portfolio project.
